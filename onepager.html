Services

  • Aircond Repair — diagnostics and fixes: cooling problems, noise, refrigerant leaks, compressor troubleshooting, fan motor replacement, gas leakage detection, electrical repair and thermostat troubleshooting.
  • Aircond Installation — wall-mounted split units and ceiling cassettes for new homes, shop lots and offices; VRV/VRF and centralised or ducted HVAC for larger premises.
  • Chemical Wash & Chemical Cleaning — deep clean of the evaporator coil and drainage, mould and bacteria removal; chemical overhaul for heavily contaminated or older units.
  • Aircond Gas Top Up — pressure check and leak diagnostic before refilling; R32 and R410A gas, matched to the unit.
  • Water Leak Repair — clearing clogged drainage pipes and checking the drain pan slope.
  • Preventive Maintenance — scheduled servicing: filter cleaning, coil inspection, gas pressure checking and drain cleaning. Residential units every six months; offices and shops every three to four months.
  • Emergency Aircond Repair — leaking or non-responsive units, attended around the clock.
  • Commercial HVAC Service — maintenance packages for offices, retail outlets, medical clinics and server rooms.
  • Residential Aircond Service — servicing for apartments, landed houses and condominiums.
  • Aircond Troubleshooting — step-by-step diagnosis before any repair is recommended.
